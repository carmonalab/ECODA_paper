## Mutation and fail-closed rules
- Evidence belongs to a phase and exact canonical path, content digest, size, selection/view, source/runtime identity, owner and alias scope; it is not a generic path cache.
- Pin descriptors/path chains; reject replacement, symlink, hardlink, nonregular files and wrong mounts. Check identity/type/link count and before/after size/mtime/ctime as mutation alarms; these checks alone never prove content equality.
- Same-size/same-mtime in-place rewrites must be detected by authoritative content verification. Across mutable write intervals, process handoffs or runs, retain a fresh hash unless existing enforceable immutable/protected custody excludes writes. No assumption that a receipt, flock, open descriptor or chmod alone freezes every external writer.
- Re-read small sidecars/control digests at final binding. Changed owner/receipt/inventory/source/pending invalidates earlier results and blocks; do not silently refresh a sealed plan or fall back to recomputation.
- Preserve no-clobber canonical publication, private predecessor quarantine, exact archived predecessor proof before replacement/deletion, preserved unexpected bytes, receipt exclusivity and interruption barriers.
- Keep NAS mount-source/selected-ancestor checks, capacity/probe, 666/777 verification, exact configured selected H5AD+sidecar scope, original source PATH in NAS sidecars, unlisted backup rejection and run-owned control paths.
- Keep strict source/runtime provenance, terminal producer/PUBLISHED evidence, ACTIVE/malformed/foreign-owner refusal, canonical-root/alias checks, current scientific shape/finite/sample-order contracts and byte-identical expected pending.
- DS metadata export `3405-3406` can submit an obs-export array (`3192`) before source repair. Distinguish **first scheduler job** from **first method job**; test both metadata-valid and export-required branches. Do not falsely claim all current checks precede every `sbatch`.
