Dear HPC Users;
We are pleased to announce the availability of a new interactive application on OpenOnDemand: LMStudio[LMStudio](https://openondemand.baobab.hpc.unige.ch/pun/sys/dashboard/batch_connect/sys/ug_LMStudio/session_contexts/new).
LMStudio is a simple and intuitive application that allows you to load, run, and interact with your language model (LLM) locally. With its user‑friendly interface, you can import a model, start a session, and begin interacting with it in just a few clicks.
It is designed to make exploring and experimenting with language models easy, without any complex configuration.
You can now launch LMStudio directly from the OpenOnDemand web interface and start working with your LLMs in just a few clicks.
