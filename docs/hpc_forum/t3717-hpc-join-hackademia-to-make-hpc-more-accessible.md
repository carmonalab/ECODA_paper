Dear HPC community,
We are pleased to invite you to participate in a unique project at the upcoming Hackademia hackathon, organized by the University of Geneva. This project, entitled Making HPC Accessible to Non-Specialist Users[Making HPC Accessible to Non-Specialist Users](https://hackademia2024.sparkboard.com/project/670672b34c622cad8b431c84) and initiated by @Victor.Ferat[@Victor.Ferat](https://hpc-community.unige.ch/u/victor.ferat), has recently been approved.
This is an ideal opportunity to come together and collaborate on solutions to make HPC more accessible to researchers and students who are not HPC experts. By working side by side, we can create tangible ideas to improve accessibility to HPC.
Whether you’re an expert in HPC or simply passionate about making the technology more accessible, your participation will be invaluable.
For more information and to register, visit the official page: Hackademia - Université de Genève[Hackademia - Université de Genève](https://www.unige.ch/hackademia/)
Here’s the Hackademia roadmap, the next important date is this Thursday, November 14, 4pm - 6:30pm for the bootcamp:
- Centre Maurice Chalumeau en science des sexualités, Uni Battelle, Bâtiment A, 7 rte de Drize
We hope to see many of you there, ready to tackle this challenge together!
