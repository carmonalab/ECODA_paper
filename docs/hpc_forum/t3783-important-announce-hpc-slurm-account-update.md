Dear HPC Users,
Following the previous email sent to PIs in December, we are currently updating HPC Slurm accounts. Please be aware that some of you may no longer be able to submit Slurm jobs with the following error message:
```
srun: error: Unable to allocate resources: Invalid account or account/partition combination specified
```
This change is due to your PI (as registered in the Slurm database) requesting the removal of your account from their group.
As account updates take time, we will send a confirmation email once all changes are applied. If you notice any issues before this communication, please fill the form on DigitalWorplace [form on DigitalWorplace](https://dw.unige.ch/openentry.html?tid=hpc) (=> request an account) and update your PI information.
If your are not able to access to the form (due to Outsider  Account) please email us with your PI in CC to update your information. This is required for HPC billing and MUST be validated by your new PI.
Than you for your understanding and your cooperation.
