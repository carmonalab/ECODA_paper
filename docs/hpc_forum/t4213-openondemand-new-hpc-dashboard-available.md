Dear HPC Users;
We are pleased to announce the release of a new HPC dashboard accessible directly via OpenOnDemand.
This dashboard has been designed to provide clear and immediate visibility into your HPC usage and help you better understand how you consume computing resources.
Feel free to start exploring it today by visiting `Accounting Tab > my_hpc_usage` in Boabab[Boabab](https://openondemand.baobab.hpc.unige.ch/) Yggdrasil[Yggdrasil](https://openondemand.yggdrasil.hpc.unige.ch/) or Bamboo[Bamboo](https://openondemand.bamboo.hpc.unige.ch/) OpenOnDemand Instance.

## What the new dashboard offers ?
### :abacus:  Quick Overview of your HPC environment
- A graphical overview of your HPC consumption over a pre-selectionned time period. (november → Now)
- Slurm account information
  - A list of all Slurm accounts you are affiliated with.
  - Clear identification of your default Slurm account, which is used automatically when no account is specified for job submissions.

### :card_index_dividers: Group and partition access overview
- A summary table of the groups  and you belong to  and associated Slurm servers/partitions you have access to.

### :chart_increasing: Intra‑PI consumption comparison
- A comparative graph showing the consumption of all users belonging to the same PI, expressed in **Billing Units **.
- Useful for understanding how computing resources are used within your research group.
