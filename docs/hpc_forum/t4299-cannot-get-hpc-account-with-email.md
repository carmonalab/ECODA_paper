## Post 2 by @Gael.Rossignol (2026-06-04T07:38:16.465Z)

Dear Augustin,
It seems that you have two accounts with different roles: an employee account (the one you are logged in with when creating the request) and a student account (which always ends with a number).
If you wish to create an HPC cluster account using your student account, you need to log in to the SI support website with that specific account.
However, we generally recommend using your employee account to create an HPC account, as this is the one you will keep if you continue working at UNIGE after your studies.
I hope this helps.
