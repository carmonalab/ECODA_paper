Dear HPC Users,
We are pleased to announce the launch of our new HPC Status Page.
This platform provides a centralized, real-time view of the status of our HPC infrastructure and services. It now replaces the “Current Issue” forum topic as the primary location for status information.
Incident, and planned maintenance will be published on the status page, providing a more accessible and up-to-date source of information.
Planned maintenance announcements will continue to be communicated through our usual channels, including email and forum announcements, in addition to being listed on the status page.
You will also find status badges for each cluster in the forum header. These badges are automatically updated and can be clicked to access the corresponding cluster status page directly.
