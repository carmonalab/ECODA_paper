Yes, you need to be added as user of the PI “on behalf of whom” and you specify this PI (account in Slurm vocabulary) when you submit your job. hpc:faq [eResearch Doc][hpc:faq [eResearch Doc]](https://doc.eresearch.unige.ch/hpc/faq#i_m_a_user_and_i_ve_noticed_th)
