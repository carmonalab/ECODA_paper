Following the issue reported here:: 🔔 Temporary Disablement of Slurm Email Notifications[🔔 Temporary Disablement of Slurm Email Notifications](https://hpc-community.unige.ch/t/temporary-disablement-of-slurm-email-notifications/4205)
In collaboration with the team in charge of the University of Geneva central mail server (Postmaster), we have implemented a mechanism to enforce quotas on Slurm email notifications.
As of today, Slurm email notifications have been re-enabled with a limit of 100 emails per hour per user. :incoming_envelope:
These settings have been designed to preserve the stability of the University of Geneva email infrastructure while still allowing users to monitor the status of their jobs.
Thank you for your understanding.
